# Report pages

The report has 15 pages. Most share a standard slicer bar (**month, equipment, sales team, quoting method, shipper**), and every page excludes months under $1 M of bookings.

## 1. Orientation

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Instructions** | How MoM growth splits into ΔPrice and ΔVolume, the four quadrants and their KPI weights, with a worked example: an animated scatter you can *Play* month by month. It uses the quadrants' earlier names (Growth, Profitable, Loss Leader, Strategic); see [methodology](methodology.md#10-notes--caveats). |
| **Executive Summary** | One-page view for leadership: Pricing Power, sourcing spread, RPM this vs. last month, bookings, ΔPrice and ΔVolume, plus the share of lanes and loads per quadrant over time. |

## 2. KPI

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Summary** | Current-month cards for the company, then broken down by equipment, sales team and quoting method: bookings, ΔPrice, ΔVolume, KPI, sourcing spread and share of loads. A donut splits bookings or sourcing spread (slicer) across the shipper KPI buckets, with coverage indicators. |
| **KPI Composition** | Monthly lanes and loads per quadrant (lines and 100% stacked columns) against Pricing Power and the company-wide KPI. |
| **KPI Evol.** | KPI, ΔPrice, ΔVolume and MoM over time; amount and number of shippers per KPI bucket over time. |
| **Segments Evol.** | Pricing Power vs. company-wide KPI, one line chart each by quoting method, sales team and equipment. |

## 3. Price & volume

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **DP/DV Evol.** | Monthly bookings, mileage and RPM, current vs. previous month, by sales team and equipment; ΔPrice / ΔVolume / MoM lines; lane-level table. |
| **Big Figures** | Company totals with the two decomposition sanity checks, an RPM-by-distance-band area chart and a box-and-whisker of lane RPM distribution (AppSource visual). |

## 4. Lanes

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Scatter Plot** | Every KMA lane plotted by ΔVolume (x) and ΔPrice (y): the four quadrants made visible. Bubble size is loads; tooltips show conversion, markup, carriers, shippers and quadrant volatility. |
| **Scatter Plot [Table]** | The same lanes as a sortable matrix: quadrant, ΔPrice/ΔVolume, MoM, RPM this vs. last month, conversion (month and trailing 12 months), markup, carriers, volatility and Lane Score. |
| **Lane Evol.** | One lane over time: bookings, markup and conversion; its quadrant month by month; how often it changed quadrant. |
| **Lane Comparison** | Lanes side by side with their quadrant this month, last month and next month, plus volatility. Shows which lanes moved and where to. |
| **Lane Scores** | Two tables: lanes graded **A** (sourcing spread > 5% two months running) and lanes graded **D/F** (below −3%), with markup, conversion and loads. |
| **Reports** | Export-style tables: by shipper (KPI, ΔPrice, ΔVolume, MoM, spread, markup) and by lane. |

## 5. Shippers

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Shippers** | Shipper table with KPI, sourcing spread and Lane Score; shippers and amount per KPI bucket; lanes and loads per quadrant for the selection. |

## Reading the pages together

1. **Summary** and **KPI Evol.** show whether this month's growth came from price or volume, and how the KPI compares with the company line.
2. **Segments Evol.** shows which channel, team or equipment is driving it.
3. **Scatter Plot** shows which lanes those are, and **Lane Comparison** whether they're moving.
4. **Shippers** and **Lane Scores** turn that into an account-level conversation: who to re-price and where margin is leaking.
